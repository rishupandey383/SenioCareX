SeniorCareX – Smart Elderly Care & Health Support Platform

SeniorCareX is a software solution designed to assist elderly individuals and their caregivers by centralizing health monitoring, medication management, and emergency support in a single platform.
The goal of the project is to improve daily care coordination, reduce missed medications, and provide timely assistance during emergencies.

Key Features

User Management – Separate roles for seniors, caregivers, and family members

Medication Reminders – Scheduled alerts to ensure medicines are taken on time

Health Records Management – Store and view basic medical history and health data

Emergency Assistance – Quick-access emergency alert system for critical situations

Caregiver Monitoring – Enables caregivers to track patient status remotely

Simple & Accessible UI – Designed for ease of use by elderly users

Tech Stack

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Backend:  Node.js (use what you actually used — do not lie)

Database: MySQL

Objective

The objective of SeniorCareX is to provide a reliable, easy-to-use digital care companion for senior citizens while giving caregivers better visibility and control over routine health-related activities.
